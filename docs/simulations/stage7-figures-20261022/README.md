# Fixed-seed component illustrations

The [PNG](synthetic_recovery.png) and [PDF](synthetic_recovery.pdf) were visually
inspected. The [audit](audit.json) binds the plotting source, routed records,
eligibility decisions and rendered files. These four completed cases use the
predeclared seed 7101, not the best-performing seed. Their records already
appear in the completed component subset; the plots do not claim that the
whole campaign was complete when they were generated.

The factor panel shows one dense category's latent log intensity and a pointwise
90% posterior interval. The regime panel is retrospective recovery of an
obvious fixed synthetic regime path, not an out-of-sample warning experiment.
The dispersion panel conditions on the true baseline level, giving separation
a favorable setting; it illustrates dependence between the two noise components.

The kernel panel retains the numerical-pass/failed-exploration distinction and
withholds recovery. Its unresolved-region label does not estimate posterior
region probabilities. Failures and exclusions for every seed remain in the
case tables; this four-panel illustration is not a calibration estimate.
